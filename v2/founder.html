Jamal Yusuf Al Aseeri has over three decades of professional experience in media, public relations, strategic communications, government communications, and media and marketing advisory services. Throughout his career, he has held senior positions in prominent government and media institutions in the Kingdom of Bahrain, managed media coverage of major national events and developments, and contributed to the establishment and development of several media and communications organisations. He also provides specialised advisory services to major private-sector companies.

One of the most significant chapters of his career was his appointment as Media Advisor at the Royal Court of the Kingdom of Bahrain, a position he held from 2004 to 2023. Over this 19-year period, he developed extensive experience in government communications at a senior institutional level, building on his earlier career in journalism, broadcasting, newsroom management, and media operations.

Alongside his government experience, Al Aseeri provides media, communications, and marketing advisory services to major private-sector companies. His work covers public relations, corporate communications, media and marketing planning, communications strategy, media relations, and corporate reputation management. His advisory work is supported by extensive experience in media relations, corporate communications, and the development of communications strategies aligned with business objectives and stakeholder expectations.

Al Aseeri began his professional career at the Gulf News Agency and subsequently joined the Bahrain News Agency, where he served from 1994 to 2004. He progressed through several editorial positions, from News Editor to Senior News Editor and ultimately Chief News Editor. These roles provided him with extensive experience in journalism, newsroom management, editorial operations, and the coordination of news coverage.

In parallel with his work in news agencies, he served as a news and current affairs programme producer and presenter at Bahrain Television from 1995 to 2004. He was also an accredited part-time correspondent for the British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC) in Bahrain between 2003 and 2006, combining experience in national broadcasting with professional engagement in international media.

His career also included significant responsibilities in covering key developments in Bahrain's parliamentary and political institutions. During the early years of Bahrain's Shura Council, beginning in 1993, he supervised its media coverage and subsequently oversaw media coverage of both the Shura Council and the Council of Representatives from 2002 to 2004. These responsibilities provided him with specialised experience in parliamentary communications and legislative affairs reporting.

In 2002, Al Aseeri was seconded by the Executive Committee for Elections to serve as Media Director for Bahrain's first parliamentary elections following the resumption of parliamentary life. In this capacity, he managed media operations associated with a major milestone in the Kingdom's political and institutional development.

He also contributed to the development of Bahrain's media sector through membership in several committees and official bodies. In 2013, he was appointed as a member of Bahrain's Higher Authority for Media and Communication by Royal Order. He also served on the Committee for Administrative and Technical Performance Development at the Ministry of Information's Radio and Television Authority from 2002 to 2004.

His institutional contributions extended to participation in the founding committees of several major media and communications initiatives, including Bahrain's Al Watan newspaper in 2003 and Zajil PR in 2007.

A particularly significant milestone in his career was his involvement in establishing Bahrain's National Communication Centre (NCC) in 2016. Working closely with the founding team, Al Aseeri contributed to developing the Centre's initial institutional concept, organisational structure, media operating framework, and operational strategies.

The NCC was established as a specialised government institution responsible for developing and coordinating government communications across the Kingdom of Bahrain. Its responsibilities include coordinating media activities among ministries and government entities, developing government communications strategies and plans, and promoting consistency in official messaging. Al Aseeri's involvement in its establishment included direct contributions to the development of an integrated government communications framework, encompassing institutional, organisational, strategic, and operational components.

Al Aseeri also served as a lecturer in the Department of Media at the University of Bahrain from 1999 to 2000. He was a member of the jury at the Cairo Radio and Television Festival in 2003 and has participated in numerous political and media conferences, seminars, and professional development programmes in Bahrain and internationally.

Al Aseeri holds a Bachelor's degree in Media Studies from the College of Media and Communication at Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
