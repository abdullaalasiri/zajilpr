Zajil PR was founded in early 2008 to meet growing demand for a modern media-focused PR agency with practical, journalistic experience of the regional, national and international press. As such, the agency acts as a bridge between its clients and the wider telecom operators, print and digital media by delivering newsworthy and original PR campaigns. Zajil PR was launched with a focus on generating unparalleled levels of global media coverage in the B2B PR and consumer PR sectors. Today, its services have evolved to include targeted exposure, brand reputation development and PR consultancy at the highest level.

Our experience of the national and international media means the PR copy we produce usually appears in the national and international print and/or online word-for-word and without the need for an edit – Safeguarding the success of the campaign by protecting name-checks, links which might otherwise be removed. The strategy – of setting the news agenda, through client-driven stories and/or PR stunts – has generated thousands of page leads worldwide, won awards, and continues to drive business growth for start-ups, SMEs and larger companies. Zajil PR specializes in public relations across several industry, and has the credentials to prove it. But the agency’s real expertise lies in its ability to secure wide-scale media coverage for brands in any sector. This is because Zajil PR is one of few Bahraini agencies to rely upon the experience of former national and International news journalists – a prerequisite, we believe, for a successful PR campaign.
