Our History

Zajil PR was founded in early 2008 to meet growing demand for a modern media-focused PR agency with practical, journalistic experience of the regional, national and international press.

As such, the agency acts as a bridge between its clients and the wider telecom operators, print and digital media by delivering newsworthy and original PR campaigns. Zajil PR was launched with a focus on generating unparalleled levels of global media coverage in the B2B PR and consumer PR sectors. Today, its services have evolved to include targeted exposure, brand reputation development and PR consultancy at the highest level.
